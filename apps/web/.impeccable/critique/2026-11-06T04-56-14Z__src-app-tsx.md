## Overall Impression
The objects are wonderful; the paths between them are not. The journey the brand is built on (see a piece on Facebook, claim, find, pay, feel it's yours) breaks where money and a 30-minute clock are involved. Biggest opportunity: make the claim real on the site.

## What's Working
1. The rack as a true signature (RackPiece.tsx): hanger through the collar, tag swinging a beat later.
2. Honest about what isn't decided, in brand voice: "Price at opening", placeholders with shot briefs, "(coming soon)".
3. Specific local payment copy; the Evening theme flips cleanly.
